# REQ-HIST-001: Show recorded transactions to participants

Reference requirement: synthetic and suitable for public demonstration. It is not a transcription of RWA Cypress tests.

## Business intent
Participants can review the transactions associated with their accounts.

## Risk level
MEDIUM

## Preconditions
- The user is authenticated.

## Acceptance criteria
- A sender can find a recorded payment in their transaction history.
- A recipient can find a received payment in their transaction history.

## Explicit unknowns
- Transaction ordering, retention duration, and pagination behavior are not specified.
